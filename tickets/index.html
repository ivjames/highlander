TICKETS

See a show.

Tickets are sold online through Ludus. When a show is on sale, its event link takes you straight to checkout — on desktop or mobile.

No tickets are on sale right now. When events are announced they'll appear here and open in the Ludus box office.

How ticketing works

Ludus is the auditorium's online box office — a flexible buying experience built for fast checkout on desktop or mobile. Selecting an event link redirects you to Ludus.com to complete your purchase.

  • Buy online and print at home, or receive digital tickets by SMS, Apple Wallet, or email.
  • Credit cards are accepted online; cash is accepted at the box office.
  • Tickets are scanned and validated at the door.

Before you buy

All sales are final, so review the ticket policies first. To choose where you'd like to sit, see the seating chart — most events are general admission, first come, first served.
