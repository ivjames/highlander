TICKET POLICIES

Ticket policies.

Please review these policies before purchasing. Buying a ticket means you accept them.

All sales final

All sales are final — no refunds or exchanges. No refund applies if a performance is rescheduled. If an event is canceled and not rescheduled, you may receive a refund.

Seating

Unless an event is reserved seating, seating is first come, first served. Every attendee, regardless of age, requires their own seat and ticket. Latecomers are seated at the discretion of the house manager or staff, at an appropriate pause in the performance.

At the door

  • A valid ID is required for all staff and student discounted tickets, when applicable.
  • Please turn off all electronic devices during performances.
  • Food and drink are not allowed in the auditorium or lobby.

Fees

A charge of 5% plus $0.75 per ticket is added for credit-card processing and service fees.

Health

Masks are recommended but not required. Please don't attend if you're feeling ill.

Assumption of risk

The ticket holder assumes all risks, hazards, and dangers occurring before, during, or after the event, including injury by any cause.
