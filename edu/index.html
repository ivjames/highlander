EDUCATIONAL EVENTS

Educational events.

The auditorium hosts Upland Unified School District school and educational events throughout the year, booked through the district.

Educational events are scheduled through the district. Once your date is confirmed, complete the booking request and use the shared resource folder to plan your event.

  • Confirm your event date with the district.
  • Complete the EDU booking request form once the date is set.
  • Use the event resource folder to prepare technical and logistical details.

Questions about an educational booking? Contact the auditorium.
