ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility.

The auditorium aims to aid and assist all reasonable requests for guests with disabilities.

Getting in and around

  • Wheelchair seating is located at the rear of the house, on either side.
  • A wheelchair entrance is available at the main lobby entrance.
  • Accessible parking is in the primary lot, with ramped access to the house entrances.

Have a request we haven't covered here? Please contact us and we'll do our best to accommodate.
