ABOUT

About the auditorium.

A 1,073-seat proscenium theatre in Upland, California, operated by Upland Unified School District.

Highlander Auditorium is a full-production proscenium theatre 30 minutes east of downtown Los Angeles. With 1,073 seats, ETC lighting, a Yamaha console, and a 27-foot HD screen, it hosts ticketed performances, district and educational events, and private rentals.

Our philosophy

The auditorium's stated philosophy puts the guest at the center of everything it does:

  • “Our” Client is…the most important person in our business.
  • “Our” Customer is…a human being with feelings like our own.
  • “Our” Patron…deserves our most courteous and attentive treatment.
  • “Our” Client…does not interrupt our work: he is the purpose of it.
  • “Our” Customer…brings us his needs; it is our job to fulfill them.
  • “Our” Patron…is part of our business; he is not an outsider.
  • “Our” Client…does not depend on us; we depend on him.

A Client “IS” our business.

The venue is operated by Upland Unified School District, serving its schools and the wider community.

The auditorium is run by a part-time team of over 20 entertainment professionals — meet the staff.
